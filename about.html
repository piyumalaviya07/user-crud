About Us

At Coding, we are passionate about creating beautiful, responsive, and modern websites. Our goal is to educate and empower learners to understand the complete process of web design and development.

We provide training in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Responsive Design, UI/UX principles, layout designing, and web development workflows. Whether you are a beginner or want to improve your skills, our course curriculum is designed to guide you step-by-step.
